#FollowtheMoneyCO

Colorado state campaign finance data, made searchable for journalists and the public. Contributions and spending for statewide, state House and state Senate candidates, plus super PACs and issue committees, drawn from the Secretary of State’s TRACER system.

Most recent update: Aug. 4. The next filing date is Sept. 8. This is the last update that will include defeated primary election candidates.

Independent spending was also updated Aug. 4. Expenditures naming multiple candidates were divided equally among them.

Tables ending in _cont are contributions; those ending in _exp are expenses. For custom queries, see using SQL. For what the data covers and how it is built, see more info.
